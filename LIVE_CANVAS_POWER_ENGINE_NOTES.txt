PUNJABI GAME ARENA — PROCEDURAL LIVE POWER ENGINE

This update replaces the image-based projectile layer with a real-time HTML Canvas 2D renderer. The selected power is not displayed as a PNG. Its orb/core, trails, arcs, rings, sparks, particles, collision and impact are drawn procedurally on every requestAnimationFrame.

Preserved:
- Turn-by-turn power selection (Player 1 then Player 2)
- Hidden first-player selection during Player 2's turn
- Existing player name editing, hero selection, Attack button, round progression and winner flow
- Existing power-strength comparison, shield mitigation, healing and damage values
- Existing fighter artwork and arena background
- No keyboard or free-roam controls

Live animation:
- Charge: energy core grows at each fighter's hand
- Release: both selected powers travel toward the centre with a live trail
- Collision: animated energy ring and competing projectiles compress
- Breakthrough: losing energy collapses; winning energy travels from the centre to the opponent's body
- Impact: procedural radial shockwave and sparks; fighter hit reaction plays
- Health is reduced at the visual impact moment (6.25 seconds), not after the recovery delay
- Recovery: the existing round result appears after the full 8.25-second sequence

Power-specific procedural shapes are drawn for lightning, fire, ice, water, shadow, solar, plasma, shield, healing, wind, impact and speed families. No power-effect PNG is rendered by the live combat component.
