This build uses the user-uploaded animatedOgreWith6Clips model for the left warrior slot and animatedKnightInPlateArmour for the right knight slot. The first model is an OGRE-style fantasy humanoid, not a historically realistic human Spartan. The Knight includes Idle, Walk, Walk_Root, Run, Run_Root, Attack, Hit, Jump, Kick. The Ogre includes Idle, Walk, Walk_Root, Run, Run_Root, Attack, Hit, GroundSlam. Both are rigged GLBs. Animation-driven death is not included; game fall behaviour blends Hit with a procedural root tilt/drop. Licenses and credits are preserved under public/models/licenses/.
